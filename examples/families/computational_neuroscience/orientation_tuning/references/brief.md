# Brief: orientation tuning in primary visual cortex

Explain, for a graduate neuroscience audience, that neurons in primary visual cortex respond most strongly to
one orientation of a bar or grating and less to others, that this tuning is summarised by a bell-shaped
curve over orientation, and that a von Mises function is a common model of it. Show the recording site's
circuit schematically (stimulus, V1 population, readout, with inhibition), one measured-looking tuning
curve, the model equation, and the model curve, with the stimulus shown as a grating whose orientation turns
while a marker follows it on the model curve. All data are synthetic.

## Sources

`data/tuning.csv` holds toy tuning curves for two conditions (`control`, `adapted`) computed for this
example; they are not measurements.
